QUBE INDUSTRIAL SYSTEM: ENERGY OPTIMIZATION KNOWLEDGE BASE v1.0

[SECTION 1: GLOBAL ELECTRICAL STANDARDS]
- Ideal Power Factor (PF): 0.95 to 1.00.
- Marginal Power Factor (PF): 0.85 to 0.90. Requires tuning of capacitor banks.
- Critical Power Factor (PF): Below 0.80. Results in kVA surcharges and overheating of conductors.
- Maximum Demand Limit: Do not exceed 90% of Contract Demand (kVA) to avoid utility penalties.

[SECTION 2: ASSET-SPECIFIC MAINTENANCE & SPECS]

DEVICE: Electrical-WWTP (Water Treatment Plant)
- Equipment: 3x 15kW Submersible Pumps.
- Optimal PF: 0.92.
- Failure Mode: PF below 0.50 usually indicates a stuck non-return valve causing motor "no-load" idling or faulty primary inductor coils.
- Recommendation: Install 50kVAR Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) panel.
- Maintenance: Inspect pump seals every 2,000 operational hours.

DEVICE: Electrical-Air-Compressor
- Equipment: Rotary Screw Compressor (Atlas Copco GA-series equivalent).
- Optimal Usage: 7-bar pressure constant.
- Peak Shaving: Sequence start-ups with 5-minute delays to avoid inrush current spikes above 100kVA.
- Waste Alert: If consumption > 5kWh during non-production hours, check for pneumatic line leaks.

DEVICE: Electrical-Boiler
- Equipment: Industrial Steam Generator.
- Optimal PF: 0.98 (Resistive heating).
- Efficiency Logic: If Peak kVA exceeds 3x Average kWh, inspect heating element insulation.
- Service Step: Descale heat exchanger every 6 months to maintain thermal efficiency.

DEVICE: Electrical-Refrigeration
- Equipment: Industrial Ammonia Chiller Plant.
- Critical Limit: Peak Demand must not exceed 1200kVA.
- Optimization: Shift defrost cycles to 10 PM - 6 AM window to reduce peak-hour kVA charges.
- Maintenance: Inspect compressor oil levels monthly.

[SECTION 3: REMEDIATION ACTIONS]
- Capacitor Banks: Used to correct low Power Factor. Sizing = (kW) * [tan(acos(PF_current)) - tan(acos(PF_target))].
- Peak Shaving: The process of reducing load during peak utility hours. Implementation: Install VFDs (Variable Frequency Drives) on 22kW+ motors.
- Load Sequencing: Programming PLC logic to ensure high-drain assets (Chillers + Compressors) do not start simultaneously.
